# Publishing your first CV

This guide walks you from an empty Master Resume to a public, shareable CV link.

## 1. Edit your Master Resume

1. Open **Dashboard** in the top navigation, then choose **Edit Master Resume** (or go directly to `/master-resume`).
2. The editor shows a form on the left and a **live preview** on the right. Every change you make updates the preview immediately.
3. Fill in your core data: name, job title, professional summary, contact details, skills, work experience, and education.
4. Your Master Resume is the single source of truth — you will never publish it directly. Instead, you publish selected snapshots of it.

## 2. Create a CV Version

1. In the editor, find the **Saved Versions and public links** section.
2. Create a new version: give it a title and select which entries from your Master Resume it should include. This selection is what a viewer of that version will see — anything you leave unselected stays private.
3. Save it. New versions start as **Private** with a **Noindex** badge: nothing is public yet.

## 3. Publish the version

1. Click **Publish** next to your version.
2. In the publish dialog, choose the language versions to include and the **default locale**.
3. Decide whether search engines may index the CV with the **Allow indexing** toggle. You can change this later.
4. Confirm. The badges switch to **Published** (and **Indexable** if you allowed indexing).

## 4. Share the public link

1. After publishing, use **Copy public URL** to copy your canonical link, or **Open public CV** to preview it as a visitor.
2. The link format is `/{your-slug}/{public-id}` and always shows the published snapshot — later edits to your Master Resume are not visible until you publish again.
3. To take the CV offline, use **Unpublish** on the same version. The link stops resolving immediately.

## Next steps

- Add a second language version and republish to serve the CV in both languages.
- Use the **ATS Ready** export options on the dashboard to download recruiter-friendly `.txt` or `.yaml` variants.
