# OpenCiVera — Product Definition

## Register
**brand** — landing page and marketing surfaces are core to the product identity.

## Users
- **Primary**: Tech-savvy professionals (software engineers, technical writers, data scientists) who value structure and control over their public career narrative
- **Secondary**: Hiring teams and recruiters looking for sharper, more intentional CV signals
- **Tertiary**: Language-conscious professionals in EN/PL markets

## Product Purpose
OpenCiVera is a **technical premium resume publishing platform** that treats the CV as a structured, reusable data layer — not a static document. Users maintain one YAML-powered source record and publish role-aware, locale-specific public surfaces from it.

Core value proposition:
- **One source, many surfaces**: Maintain a single master resume; publish different public narratives for different contexts
- **Structure over editing**: Data-driven, not document-driven
- **Language-aware delivery**: EN/PL locale support built into the publishing model
- **Publication control**: Intentional release cycles, not ad-hoc file branching

## Tone & Voice
- **Technical, not marketing-speak**: Speak like an engineer to engineers
- **Clarity over cleverness**: Prefer direct statements to metaphors
- **Opinionated but not dogmatic**: Show conviction in the publishing model without dismissing alternatives
- **Precise terminology**: "master source", "publication", "surface", "locale" — these words matter

## Brand Principles
1. **Structure is beauty**: The design reflects the underlying data model. No gratuitous ornament
2. **Light control, maximum clarity**: Users own the signal. Respect their data and their choices
3. **Platform mindset, not template**: We're building a system, not selling a theme
4. **Bilingual integrity**: EN/PL are first-class, not afterthoughts

## Anti-references
❌ SaaS cream (glossy, overstuffed marketing pages)
❌ Crypto aesthetics (neon, extreme contrast, illegibility)
❌ Minimalism overreach (empty space pretending to be elegance)
❌ Document-tool UX (rich text editors, WYSIWYG, "templates")

## Visual Direction
- **Palette**: Dark technical aesthetic (near-black backgrounds, indigo primary, teal secondary)
- **Typography**: Editorial-grade hierarchy (Sora for display, DM Sans for body, JetBrains Mono for data)
- **Imagery**: Abstract system diagrams and data flow visualizations, not lifestyle photography
- **Motion**: Purposeful, restrained — pulse animations for live states, smooth scroll anchoring for navigation
- **Density**: Information-rich without clutter. Cards and grids, but never nested cards

## Success Metrics
- Landing page communicates the publishing model in <90 seconds
- Users understand the EN/PL localization feature
- Technical audience sees the structural ambition (not just another resume builder)
- CTA drives traffic to login and sample public CV
